# Roadmap change log (full history)

This is the complete, append-only change log for `docs/roadmap.md`, moved
here on 2026-08-29 (Track 3 of `docs/repo-review-2026-08-29.md`) because it
had grown to roughly 40% of that file's size — every agent following
`AGENTS.md`'s mandatory "read `docs/roadmap.md` in full" instruction was
paying that cost on every task, most of which need only current status, not
the full history of how it was reached.

Nothing here was edited or shortened; every entry is exactly as it was
recorded in `docs/roadmap.md`. `docs/roadmap.md` keeps its own copy of the
most recent entries for at-a-glance recent context; this file is the
complete record and the one to append future entries to. Do not renumber,
reorder, or edit a past entry — append new ones at the end, matching the
maintenance contract in `docs/roadmap.md`'s "How to maintain this roadmap"
section.
